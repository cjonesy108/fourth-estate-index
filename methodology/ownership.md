# Media ownership layer

Answers *who owns the outlet*. Separate from journalist SPJ scores.

## Two lenses

1. **Voting control** — dual-class families, private beneficial owners, trusts. Who can fire the editor or sell the company.
2. **Economic stake** — 13F institutional holdings in the *public parent*. Capital exposure. Not editorial control unless a 13D, board seat, or dual-class table says otherwise.

## What we will not do

- Recurse into who owns BlackRock / Vanguard / State Street.
- Treat index-fund ownership as bias.
- Fold ownership into a journalist’s composite score.
- Attribute an institution’s PAC to CNN.

## Recursion cap

Operating chain: outlet → division → parent, until a controller or a widely held public issuer.

Economic chain: one hop — institution → public media issuer. Stop.
